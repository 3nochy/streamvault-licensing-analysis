| Q1 | Investigation | Content Acquisition | F17 | What are the licensing prices for the shortlisted franchise bundles and standalone titles? | The supplied dataset contains historical theatrical revenue, not licensing prices. The $14 million budget alone is insufficient to determine affordability. | Shortlisted candidates for negotiation review. Final affordability assessment requires quoted prices and rights terms. |
| Q2 | Investigation | Content Acquisition | F12 | Do TMDb collections correspond to the franchise bundles offered by rights holders, and which titles can be licensed individually? | The available data establishes TMDb collection membership only. It does not establish rights-holder package structures or individual licensing availability. | Following ADR-002, used collection membership as a franchise proxy and classified titles without recorded collection membership as `standalone_proxy`. Actual licensing arrangements require confirmation. |
| Q3 | Investigation | Finance | F13 | Should pre-2000 theatrical revenue be adjusted for inflation or historical reporting differences? | The available brief raises concerns about historical revenue but does not specify an adjustment method. The analysis uses unadjusted theatrical revenue. Across all years, franchise median revenue is approximately $578.9 million versus $161.8 million for standalone proxies. From 2000 onward, the corresponding medians are approximately $786.6 million and $283.2 million. | Following ADR-003, reported both periods separately and documented the limitation. Franchise median revenue remains higher in both periods, so the direction of this comparison does not reverse. |
| Q4 | Investigation | Content Acquisition | F17 | Which territories, platforms, exclusivity conditions, and licensing periods must the deals cover? | These commercial requirements are not specified in the available brief or dataset. | Identified these terms as unresolved requirements for comparing offers and assessing affordability. |
| Q5 | Investigation | Content Acquisition | F17 | Should acquisition priorities be based on historical theatrical revenue, audience reception, expected streaming demand, or another business objective? | The brief requires a comparison but does not prescribe a ranking metric. Historical theatrical revenue is available; licensing cost and streaming performance are not. ADR-004 records the analytical ranking method selected for this assessment. | Ranked franchise collections by total theatrical revenue and standalone proxies by individual theatrical revenue, using ratings and vote counts as supporting context. The ranking remains a proxy for acquisition review, not a measure of expected streaming value. |
| Q6 | Investigation | Content Acquisition | F16 | How should director and lead-actor performance influence acquisition decisions? | The dataset provides historical performance signals, not evidence that a particular director or actor caused a film's revenue. Robert Downey Jr. has a median theatrical revenue of approximately $1.405 billion across seven films in this dataset. The Russo brothers each have two overlapping directing credits with a median revenue of approximately $2.498 billion. | Reported descriptive performance summaries, used the first TMDb cast entry as the lead-actor proxy, and limited the summary to people with at least two films. These signals are contextual rather than causal. |
| Q7 | Investigation | Content Acquisition | F14, F17 | Which franchise candidate should receive priority for negotiation review, and what should determine its offer price? | The Avengers Collection ranks first by total theatrical revenue among the shortlisted collections, at approximately $7.92 billion across four films. Its median film revenue is approximately $1.79 billion. These figures do not establish its licensing value. | Prioritized Avengers for negotiation review based on the available historical revenue evidence. Any offer must be evaluated against the $14 million budget using the quoted bundle price and confirmed rights scope, territory, exclusivity, and licensing term. No specific offer price can be justified from the available data alone. |

Recommend adding an optional source field to future intake so Adjoa can trace candidates and review source quality. |
